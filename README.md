## Building now

**[PayStage](https://paystage.xyz)** — the check that runs before an AI agent pays. An independent, read-only
preflight for x402: `pay`, `review` or `do_not_pay` with the rule that fired, plus a receipt for every decision.
*In plain words.* Your AI assistant can buy things on its own. PayStage checks each purchase first:

- does it fit the amount you allowed, per purchase and per day?
- is it going to someone on your list?
- if not — stop it, or ask you first.

You get a receipt for every decision. Like a company card with a limit that is actually checked.

Open source, no custody, built on Base.

**[VouchGuard](https://vouchguard.app)** — the same limits in a form a person can hold. A spending mandate on an
Apple Wallet pass: per-purchase cap, per-24h ceiling, expiry and revoke. *In plain words.* AI assistants spend money all day, and the first most companies hear of it is the invoice.
VouchGuard gives each one a budget you can see:

- **a limit per purchase** — one call can't clear the account
- **a limit per day** — a busy day can't become a bill
- **who it may pay, and until when** — one job, or two weeks
- **one tap to stop it** — and anyone can scan the pass to check it is real

It lives in the wallet on your phone as a pass. No dashboard to remember.

A record of authority, not a payment card.
