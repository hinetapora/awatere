What is a machine allowed to do with money?

Two projects, both an attempt at that question. I care about the unglamorous middle of tech — the parts that decide whether a number can be checked rather than whether it sounds good.

In progress

PayStage — the check that runs before an agent pays

An independent, read-only preflight for x402 payments. Before an AI agent signs anything it gets back pay, review or do_not_pay, with the rule that fired — caps, velocity windows, allow-lists, and a receipt for every decision.

Open source · no custody · no keys held · built on Base

VouchGuard — the same limits, in a form a person can hold

A spending mandate on an Apple Wallet pass: per-purchase cap, a per-24h ceiling, an expiry and a revoke. The person paying can see what a teammate may spend without opening a dashboard, and a counterparty can scan it and read the same state.

A record of authority, not a payment card · open source under MIT · rebrandable for your own business
